# Commander Design

Commanders are the player's persistent identity. They can be unlocked with earned currency and leveled for cosmetics, animation flair, lore, profile badges and **horizontal** Mastery choices.

## Match readability
Every Commander shows exactly:
- **Passive:** one sentence.
- **Command:** one button, once per Chapter.

No Commander level may grant hidden extra Health, cards, Power, or other ranked advantage. Sidegrades change *how* the power works, not how much money the player spent.

There are 24 core Commanders: three per element. The first of each element is a free starter. See `data/commanders.json`.
