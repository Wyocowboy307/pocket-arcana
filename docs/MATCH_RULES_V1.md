# Match Rules v1 — kid-readable core

## The sentence
**Build your magical world, win two Chapters, or break the rival Heart first.**

## Match
- 7×5 shared board.
- Each player has a Heart Sanctuary on their back edge.
- Hearts start at 25 and persist all match. Healing can raise a Heart to 30.
- First player to earn 2 Chapter Seals wins. Heart at 0 wins immediately. 10 Wonder also wins immediately.

## Hand and Chapters
- Start match with 8 cards.
- Chapter 2: draw 3. Chapter 3: draw 2.
- Cards left in hand carry into the next Chapter. This is why passing matters.
- When your rival Passes, you get **one last turn**, then the Chapter scores.
  Without that bound the player who passed first handed the other free turns
  forever, and since Shaping costs no cards those turns were pure permanent
  Realm Score — which made passing first strictly bad.
- Realm Score is deliberately simple in the graybox: creature Power + landmark Presence + 1 per shaped/special tile you control.
- Terrain and landmarks persist. Creatures retreat/clear between Chapters in v1.

## Turn
Do exactly one main action:
1. Play one card.
2. Shape one tile.
3. Move/fight with one creature.
4. Use your Command (once each Chapter).
5. Pass for the rest of the Chapter.

Then the other player acts. A player who has passed is skipped.

## Aether and Attunement
- Aether pays card costs and refills on your turn. The cap grows as the Chapter continues.
- Your Commander's Sanctuary starts as their element so a mono starter deck can play immediately.
- Shaped/special terrain provides elemental Attunement. A dual-element card requires both of its elements represented in your realm.

## Creatures
- Occupy one tile.
- Move one orthogonal tile when chosen as your action.
- Moving into an enemy creature creates simultaneous combat.
- A creature standing **beside** the rival Sanctuary may spend its action to strike
  the Heart for its Power. Creatures never enter a Sanctuary tile.
- **Sanctuary Ward:** striking the Heart costs the attacker 2 damage from the
  Sanctuary itself. Heart pressure is a real plan; it is not free.

## Shaping
Shaping spends your whole turn, so it must actually change the tile. You cannot
re-shape a tile that already has that terrain, and you cannot shape into the
rival realm.

## Element states
Frost→Frozen · Lightning→Charged · Life→Overgrown · Fire→Burning · Water→Wet · Earth→Fortified · Wind→Windswept · Death→Haunted.

Two different states on one tile can trigger a recipe. The result becomes a special terrain and is recorded in the Codex.
