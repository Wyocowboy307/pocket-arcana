# Locked Design Decisions

1. **Easy to learn, hard to master.** A new player should know the goal after one tutorial match.
2. **Win 2 Chapters or break the Heart.** Chapter scoring creates the pass/bluff/resource tension; Heart pressure keeps aggressive decks real. 10 Wonder is an alternate victory for realm-builder decks.
3. **One main action per turn.** Play a card, Shape, move/fight with one creature, use your Command, or Pass. This keeps turns readable.
4. **The board persists.** Terrain and landmarks tell the history of the match. Creatures clear between Chapters in the first implementation.
5. **Eight literal elements.** Frost, Lightning, Life, Fire, Water, Earth, Wind, Death. No abstract old school names.
6. **Commander = one passive + one Command.** Command is once per Chapter. Mastery is horizontal, never raw paid power.
7. **Aether is predictable. Attunement is spatial.** Cards are not blocked by drawing lands; the world you Shape determines which elements you can cast.
8. **Magic leaves visible evidence.** Frozen, Charged, Overgrown, Burning, Wet, Fortified, Windswept, Haunted. Pair two states on one tile and a recipe may transform it.
9. **Do not copy another card game's board or exact rules.** Inspiration is allowed; implementation must remain Pocket Arcana.
10. **No final art before the loop is fun.** The 240 cards are a library, not an order to brute-force 240 bespoke scripts.
