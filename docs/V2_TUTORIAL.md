# Pocket Arcana V2 — First Match Tutorial

## Goal

Do not throw a new player into a random battle and explain everything at once.

The tutorial should teach by making the player perform one obvious action, then immediately showing why it mattered.

## Before battle

1. Show Mossy Mae and a tiny starter deck preview.
2. One sentence: `Build Groves. Summon Life creatures on them. Break the rival Heart.`
3. Enter board.

## Opening presentation

- Mae enters.
- Poppy enters opposite.
- both Hearts appear.
- Mae's signature Grove **grows automatically** in one lane with the full vine animation.
- Poppy's Cinder land burns into one lane.

Coach text:
`Land gives you Aether and a home for your creatures.`

No other explanation yet.

## Step 1 — draw

Animate drawing Sproutling.

Pause briefly and enlarge it.

Highlight:
- `CREATURE`
- Life crest
- `PLAY ON: GROVE`
- cost

Coach:
`Sproutling lives on a Grove.`

## Step 2 — summon

Player selects Sproutling.

Only Grove glows.

Other empty lanes show `Build land here first` only if hovered.

Player drops it on Grove.

Full summon animation.

Coach:
`Nice. Creatures stay on your land and fight across their lane.`

## Step 3 — enemy answer

Poppy plays Cinder Pup opposite Sproutling with visible card and summon animation.

Do not skip the rival's card presentation.

## Step 4 — attack

Sproutling becomes ready for tutorial purposes or advance one turn.

Drag/click Sproutling toward Cinder Pup.

Attack preview arrow appears only across that lane.

Play full attack choreography.

Coach:
`Clear the creature in a lane to reach the Heart behind it.`

## Step 5 — build second land

Show Realm Stack and Grove Realm card.

Coach:
`Building another Grove gives you more Aether.`

Player places it.

Full vine-growth land animation.

Aether capacity visibly rises.

## Step 6 — Place

Draw/offer Herbalist Hut.

Card says `PLACE • PLAY ON: GROVE`.

Only Place slots on Groves highlight.

Building construction animation.

Coach:
`Places support the creature living on that land.`

## Step 7 — Spell

Draw/offer Grow or healing spell.

Card says `SPELL • TARGET: YOUR CREATURE`.

Only valid creatures highlight.

Spell visibly travels and resolves.

## Step 8 — direct Heart attack

Script an open opposing lane.

Player attacks.

Full Heart strike animation.

Coach:
`No creature blocking this lane — hit the Heart.`

## Step 9 — fusion tease

Later in the tutorial or second scripted match, put a valid pair in play.

Show linked-rune icon.

Coach:
`These two can combine.`

Player performs first Fusion with full spectacle.

Do not teach fusion in the first 60 seconds if it interrupts understanding of Land -> Creature -> Attack.

## What the tutorial does NOT teach immediately

- all eight elements
- deckbuilding
- every keyword
- advanced lane movement effects
- terrain recipes
- competitive metagame

Those unlock after the player can finish a basic match without coaching.

## Tutorial completion test

Give the player an uncoached Life vs Fire rematch.

If they cannot answer these without guessing, tutorial/UX is not done:
- where can this creature go?
- how do I get more Aether?
- what does a Place do?
- what can this Spell target?
- how do I attack the Heart?
