# Playtest Matrix

## Five-minute test
Can a new player answer: What am I trying to do? What can I do on my turn? Why would I Pass?

## Core match tests
- Life starter vs Fire starter completes all Chapters.
- One player wins via two Seals.
- One player can win by Heart damage.
- Wonder reaches 10 only through deliberate setup, not accidentally.
- Passing early can produce a hand advantage next Chapter.
- A dual card becomes legal only after both Attunements exist.
- Recipe transformation is understandable from the two visible states.

## Accessibility tests
- 10–12-year-old tester can complete tutorial without TCG vocabulary.
- Older/new-to-games tester can read cards without tiny text.
- Color-blind play remains possible via element icon + word + shape language.

## Depth tests
- Experienced card player can explain at least two meaningful reasons to Pass.
- Two decks of the same element can pursue different plans once the pool expands.
- Commander choice changes decisions but does not invalidate cards.
