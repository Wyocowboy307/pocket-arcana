# Pixel Art Bible

- 2D top-down / slight storybook perspective. No 3D requirement.
- Design around chunky, readable forms; 32px-inspired world-object footprint, with larger cards/UI rendered cleanly above that.
- One dominant silhouette per card art.
- Keep outlines and lighting consistent across AI generators.
- Each element has recurring shape language in addition to color: Frost crystals, Lightning zigzags, Life leaves, Fire flames, Water curves, Earth blocks, Wind ribbons, Death lantern wisps/bones.
- Cute and magical beats grim. Death can be spooky-fun, not gore.
- AI art is a production assistant, not art direction. Reject generations that break perspective, palette logic or silhouette readability.
- Keep source prompts alongside card IDs in `data/card_art_prompts.json`.
